# Forks and maintenance

gp64 is a personal project. I maintain it when I have the time and interest;
updates may be irregular or stop altogether. There is no release schedule or
commitment to provide support or fix reported problems.

I am not seeking contributions and do not accept pull requests. You are welcome
to fork the repository, change it and maintain your own version under the terms
of the [GPL-3.0-or-later licence](LICENSE). Retain applicable copyright and
third-party notices when redistributing it.

If repository issues are enabled, you may report a reproducible problem there,
but a response or fix is not guaranteed. Include the revision, operating system,
build command and reproduction steps. Do not attach proprietary program files,
captured program code or data, credentials, or private host information.
See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for security reports.

For building and testing a fork, see [DEVELOPMENT.md](DEVELOPMENT.md).
