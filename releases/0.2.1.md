# gp64 0.2.1 — 2026-10-15

Two-player link play over your LAN, the modem's speaker, a window that
remembers where you left it, and smoother, faster emulation.

## Added

- Link two gp64 copies over the LAN: turn on SERIAL (a null-modem cable on COM1) or MODEM (the modem on COM2) in the F12 menu on both machines and they find each other.
- The F12 menu shows the connected peer and the COM port to choose in the game's own setup, for example `ON - DESK (COM1)`.
- The modem's speaker plays through your speakers while dialling and connecting, following the modem's speaker setting.
- The window title shows CPU usage (as a share of one core) and, while the mouse is captured, the Ctrl+F10 hint to free it.
- The window reopens where it was and at the size it was, maximized if you left it maximized.
- `gp64 --script FILE` runs an automation script that types keys and clicks the mouse at frame boundaries.
- The Windows executable has an icon and version information.

## Fixed

- Serial and modem programs no longer stall after one byte: COM1 and COM2 now raise their correct interrupts.
- The emulated CPU no longer gets stuck at a low speed after a load spike, such as opening the modem screen.
- Faster emulation of the game's screen drawing.
- Starting gp64 from a command prompt returns to the prompt straight away instead of waiting for the game to close.
- `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt` now includes the libvpx copyright notice for the built-in video encoder.

## Changed

- Live recordings of low-resolution modes are captured at twice the size (320x200 becomes 640x400) so they stay sharp when scaled, and recording encodes about twice as fast on x86-64.

## Downloads

- Windows x64: `gp64-v0.2.1-windows-x86_64.zip`
- Apple Silicon macOS: `gp64-v0.2.1-macos-arm64.tar.gz`

Each archive holds the `gp64` executable (`gp64.exe` on Windows), `LICENSE`
(GPL-3.0) and `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt`. Builds are unsigned: macOS is not
Developer ID signed or notarized; Windows is not Authenticode signed.
